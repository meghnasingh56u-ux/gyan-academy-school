INFRASTRUCTURE

Modern Learning Infrastructure

Designed to provide students with a comfortable, technology-enabled and engaging learning experience.

Computer Laboratory

Modern computer systems with internet facilities to enhance digital learning and practical skills.

Science Laboratory

Fully equipped laboratory where students perform practical experiments in Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

Library

A peaceful reading space with textbooks, reference books and story books for developing reading habits.

SPORTS & ACTIVITIES

Sports & Physical Education

We encourage students to participate in indoor and outdoor sports to develop teamwork, confidence, leadership and physical fitness.

Cricket Ground

Spacious playground for cricket practice, tournaments and annual sports events.

Dueball

Dueball develops agility, quick reflexes, teamwork, and strategic thinking while encouraging students to stay active and enjoy healthy competition.

Volleyball Court

Dedicated volleyball court for regular practice sessions and inter-house competitions.

Athletics

Running, jumping and track events improve fitness, stamina and competitive spirit.

Kho-Kho

Kho Kho improves speed, stamina, coordination, and teamwork, helping students build physical fitness and confidence through traditional sports.

Kabaddi

Kabaddi enhances strength, balance, endurance, and decision-making while teaching discipline, courage, and effective teamwork.

Yoga & Meditation

Daily yoga sessions help students improve concentration, flexibility and mental wellness.

Indoor Games

Chess, carrom and other indoor games help students develop strategic thinking and focus.

Campus Security
